    About

    A fan project: the game's own program, from your disc, runs on a recreation of the PlayStation's system libraries compiled for the web. Picture and sound are the original's, pixel for pixel.

    The English text is Project NEMO's fan translation (v0.9b3), included with their permission. Discs and saves you add stay in this browser on this computer.
