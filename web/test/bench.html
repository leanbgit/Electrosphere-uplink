Renderer benchmark

Replays one of your recordings at full speed with the enhanced renderer and measures how many fields per second your PC manages. The game runs at 60 fields per second, so anything above 60 holds full speed. Sound is off; nothing is saved.

1. Files

Checking for remembered discs…

Select the .ac3i file from your recordings folder (rec\ in the native build) together with its .card1.mcd and .card2.mcd files (hold Ctrl to select all three).

2. What to measure

(0 = until the recording ends; fields before "from" are run without drawing, which is quick)

    

3. Results

Fields/s: average speed. Slower than 60: share of fields that took longer than 1/60 s (a few during loading are normal). 99th pct and worst: time per field in milliseconds.
